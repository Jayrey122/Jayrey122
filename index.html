Major and Professional Interests

I am majoring in Computer Science and have a strong interest in web development, software engineering, educational technology, and digital design. I enjoy learning how websites and programs are created because technology can be used to share information, solve problems, and create meaningful learning experiences. Through my classes I am building skills in HTML, CSS, GitHub, GitHub Desktop, and project planning. Each new project helps me become more confident with technology and gives me a better understanding of how digital tools can be organized, designed, and shared with others.

I currently work at a high school as a special education paraprofessional. Working with students has taught me how important patience, encouragement, flexibility, and clear communication can be. Every student learns differently, and I enjoy helping students work through callenges and recognize their own progress. My experiences in special education have strengthened my desire to continue working in education and to use the knowledge I have gained to support students in a possitive and respectful way.

I am working toward earning my bachelor's degree and becoming a certified high school mathematics teacher. My goal is to teach Algebra and help students understand that mathematics can be approachable whe it is taught with clear explanation, useful tools, and step by step practice. I believe that students are more likely to succeed when they feel supported and confident enough to ask questions. I want to create a classroom where students know that it is okay to make mistakes, learn from them, and keep trying until a concept makes sense.
