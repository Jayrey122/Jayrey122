My Background

My name is Jazmine Reynolds. I am a Computer Science student, a wife, and a mother of four children. I am married to my high school sweetheart, and my family is one of my biggest sources of motivation and support. Managing school, work, family responsibilites, and personal goals can be challenging, but it has taught me the value of planning, determination, and continuing to work toward my goals even when life becomes busy.

I currently work at a high school as a special education paraprofessional. I have worked in special education for the past three years, and this experience has helped me better understand the different ways students learn, communicate, and grow. I enjoy supporting students when they feel frustrated or unsure about their work. This job has shown me how important patience, encouragement, flexibility, and clear communication are when helping students succeed.

My Goal To Teach Algebra

My long term goal is to earn my bachelor's degree, complete the necessary certification requirements, and become a high school mathematics teacher. I would especially like to teach Algebra because it is a subject that has been meaningful to me since high school. I understand that some students feel nervous about mathematics or believe they are not good at it. As a future teacher I want to help students replace that fear with confidence by explaining concepts clearly, allowing time for practice, and showing them that improvement is possible.

My interest in teaching mathematics began when I took Algebra 1 in high school. My teacher Mrs. Clifton helped realize that math could be fun and easier to understand when lessons were explained in clear steps and students had the right tools to work through problems. She made a positive impression on me because she helped students feel capable instead of overwhelmed. I hope to become a teacher like her by creating a classroom where students feel safe asking questions, making mistakes, learning from those mistakes, and celebrating their progress.

Technology and Education

I am interested in combining technology and education in my future career. Learning web development and Computer Science skills has helped me see how technology can be used to organize information, create interactive learning materials, and give students more ways to demonstrate what they know. I want to continue learning about web development, software engineering, educational technology, and digital tools so I can use them responsibly and effectively in the classroom.

I would also like to support students who are interest in tecchnology, programming, and academic competitions. I hope to help students prepare for UIL events and other activities that allow them to practice Computer Science, methematics, problem solving, and teamwork. By sharing the skills I learn in my own coursework I hope to encourage students to explore technology related careers and recognize that they can succeed in those field with practice, support, and persistance.

My Hobbies and Creativity

Outside of school and work I enjoy spending time with my family and friends. I also enjoy reading, crocheting, painting, drawing, playing video games, and creating digital art.
